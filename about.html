About Us

Who We Are

ePDFConverter is a free, privacy-first suite of browser-based PDF and image tools. We believe working with documents should be fast, simple and completely private — so we built a set of tools that never upload your files.

Our mission

Every day, millions of people need to compress a PDF, convert a document, resize a photo or add a signature. Most online tools force you to upload your files to a server you don't control, ask for an account, add watermarks, or lock useful features behind a subscription.

Our mission is to remove all of that. ePDFConverter offers genuinely free, unlimited tools that run entirely inside your browser using modern web APIs and WebAssembly. There is no upload, no account, no watermark and no hidden limit — you open a page, do the job, and close it. Your documents never leave your device.

We started with the most common document tasks people search for every day — compressing, merging, editing, converting and signing PDFs, plus resizing and compressing images — and we keep improving them and adding new ones.

How our tools work

Every tool is a static page that runs client-side code. When you select a file, the browser reads it locally, processes it, and generates the result — all on your own device.

Because nothing is ever uploaded, there is nothing for us to store, leak or misuse. Your privacy is not a feature we have to promise; it is how the tools are engineered.
