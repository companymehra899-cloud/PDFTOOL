Resize Image Online

A quick look at what image resizing does and when it helps.

Resizing an image changes its pixel dimensions, which is the fastest way to shrink a photo for sharing. You can scale by a percentage to keep the original proportions, or set an exact width, height or custom size.

People resize images when a photo is too large to send by email, too slow to upload to a website, or needs to fit a specific space like an avatar or banner. The tool supports JPG, PNG and WebP files, and each resized image downloads in the format you choose.

Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to a server, and your original files stay exactly as they were.
