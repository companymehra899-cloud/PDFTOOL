Unlock & Protect PDF Online

Take control of who can open and edit your documents.

Unlocking a PDF removes the password you own, so you can open, edit and share the document without typing a key every time. Protecting a PDF does the opposite — it locks the file with a password using strong 256-bit AES encryption, so only people with the password can open it.

When you protect a PDF you can also choose which actions to allow, such as printing, copying text and editing pages. This makes it easy to share a document while keeping control over how it is used.

Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and the original PDFs stay exactly as they were — you only download a new unlocked or protected copy.

How to Unlock or Protect a PDF
