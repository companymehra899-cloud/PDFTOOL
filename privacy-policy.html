Your Privacy Is Absolute

Privacy Policy

ePDFConverter is built around one promise: your files are processed 100% in your browser and are never uploaded to any server.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ePDFConverter ("we", "our", "us") handles your information when you use our website and tools. We have designed ePDFConverter so that every PDF and image tool runs directly inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. That is why your files never need to be sent to us.

2. 100% Browser-Based Processing — Zero File Uploads

The single most important fact about ePDFConverter is this:

  • We do not upload your files. Your PDFs and images never leave your device.
  • We do not process your files on a server. All editing, merging, compressing, converting and signing happens locally in your browser.
  • We do not store your files. There is no copy of your document on any of our infrastructure, because your document is never transmitted to us in the first place.
  • We do not track file content. Since files stay on your device, we cannot see, read, analyse or share what you work with.

If you close the browser tab mid-task, your work is gone instantly and cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

3. Data We Collect

Because your files never reach our servers, the only data involved is standard website usage information, collected by third-party analytics and advertising services described below. This data is anonymous and does not identify you personally:

  • Usage analytics: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and aggregate interaction statistics, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Advertising identifiers: if ads are shown, standard cookie and device identifiers used by Google AdSense to serve relevant ads and measure performance.

We never collect your name, email address, or any other personal details simply by your using the tools.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

ePDFConverter does not use cookies for its own functionality. The third-party services we use (Google Analytics and Google AdSense) may place cookies or use local storage on your device to function correctly. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those providers, not by this Policy.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — to understand how the site is used and improve it. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — to display advertising. See Google's Advertising Policy.

None of these services ever receives your PDF or image files, because your files never leave your browser.

6. How We Protect Your Data

Because your files never leave your device, the strongest possible protection is built in by design. For the limited analytics data described above, we rely on industry-standard security practices and the safeguards provided by Google. The site is served over HTTPS so that all traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted in transit.

7. Children's Privacy

ePDFConverter is a general-audience website and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, no special arrangements are needed for children beyond this Policy.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your experience:

  • Your files: they exist only on your device, so you always control them — and deleting them is simply closing the tab.
  • Cookies: block or clear them in your browser settings at any time.
  • Personalised ads: opt out through Google Ads Settings.
  • Analytics: install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent data collection.

If you have any privacy questions, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of any changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page or email us at support@epdfconverter.com.
