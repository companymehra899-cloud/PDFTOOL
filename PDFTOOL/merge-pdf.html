Merge PDF Online

A quick look at what PDF merging does and when it helps.

PDF merging takes two or more documents and combines them into a single PDF file, keeping the pages in the order you choose. Instead of juggling several files, you get one document that is easy to email, store or archive.

People merge PDFs when they want to bring related documents together — for example, combining separate scanned pages, collecting receipts or forms, or bundling chapters into one file. You can add the PDFs in any order and reorder them before merging.

Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and the original PDFs stay exactly as they were.
