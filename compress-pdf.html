Compress PDF Online

A quick look at what PDF compression does and when it helps.

PDF compression shrinks the file size of a document by re-encoding the images and data inside it. The pages stay readable while the file takes up much less space, which makes it easier to email, upload or store.

People usually compress a PDF when a file is too large to send as an attachment, too slow to upload to a website, or simply filling up too much storage on a device or in the cloud. A compressed version of the same document works everywhere the original did.

Everything here is processed locally in your browser using modern web APIs. Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, so your documents stay on your device the whole time.
